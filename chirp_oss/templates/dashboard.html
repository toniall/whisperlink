FAST

~290 b/s payload

4 parallel 16-tone bands, 1.5–7.4 kHz, 34 ms symbols. Desk-to-desk.

ROBUST

~75 b/s payload

2 bands, 0.9–4.8 kHz, 58 ms symbols, RS(16). Noisy rooms, cheap speakers.

SESSION

TCP-like over air

SYN/SYN-ACK/ACK, seq numbers, selective ACK bitmap, window 2–4, FIN/RST, keepalive, auto FAST→ROBUST.

Quick test without a second device

  1. Open the room and tick Simulated peer (on by default).
  2. Press Connect — watch SYN → SYN-ACK → ACK in the log and the spectrum.
  3. Send a chat message or a small file; raise drop % to see NACKs, retransmits and the ROBUST fallback.
  4. With a mic: run Acoustic self-test to verify your speaker→mic path decodes.
